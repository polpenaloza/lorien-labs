This policy explains what personal data Lorien Labs LLC collects and what we do with it.

## What we collect

- **Messages you send us.** Your name, email address or phone number and the content of your message.
- **Purchase details.** Your name, email address, billing country and what you bought. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.
- **Technical logs.** Our hosting provider records IP addresses and request details to keep the site running and secure.

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics.

## Why we use it

We use your data to answer you, deliver what you bought, handle refunds and disputes, and meet our legal and tax obligations. We do not sell personal data.

## Who else processes it

- **Cloudflare** hosts this website.
- **Stripe** processes card payments.
- **Google (YouTube)** serves the videos embedded in our blog posts. A post's video loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, and YouTube may process your data once you play it.
- **Storefronts** we sell through, such as Ko-fi and itch.io, handle your purchase under their own privacy policies.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

## How long we keep it

We keep messages and purchase records for as long as we need them for the purposes above, and for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the email address below. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
