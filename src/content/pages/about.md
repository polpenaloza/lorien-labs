Lorien Labs LLC is a small software and media company. We do three things:

- **Build software.** We design and develop web applications for businesses.
- **Make digital products.** We sell pixel art packs and wallpapers created with our own code.
- **Produce videos.** We run No Ghosts Found, a channel about internet and tech mysteries that always ends with the real explanation. Every video gets a post on our blog with a summary of the story, our analysis and the sources behind it.
